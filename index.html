What to look at

    Model: switched piecewise-linear circuit (states iL, vC) integrated with RK4. Steady state is found with a shooting (Newton) method on one switching period. Startup starts from iL = 0, vC = 0. Theory uses ideal parts and the small-ripple approximation. Real circuits also ring at the switch node in DCM; this model does not include parasitic capacitance.
