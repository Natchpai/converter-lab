      What to look at

        Bode plot

        Control design

        Model: switched piecewise-linear circuit (states iL, vC) integrated with RK4. Steady state is found with a shooting (Newton) method on one switching period. Startup starts from iL = 0, vC = 0. Theory uses ideal parts and the small-ripple approximation. Non-ideal mode adds dead time with body diodes and a switch-node capacitance that rings in DCM. Switching, reverse recovery, gate and core (iGSE) losses are computed from the simulated waveforms on the Losses page.
